# Kalender · Bemanning · Innleid

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

Bemanning has two parts on the same days, switched beside the mode («Faste · Innleid», `crewPart`): the permanent staff, described below, and **Innleid** (`ui/innleid/InnleidParts.tsx`), the hours of each competence that remain after them. A day in Innleid is always 116 px wide (`INNLEID_W` in `kalender/zoom.ts`), so that most suppliers' names fit on a chip; the days of the permanent staff keep the width their span asks for, and the switch between the parts zooms from the one to the other with the day at the left edge kept (`switchPart` in `Kalender.tsx`). Innleid keeps the date header and the project lines; under them the Avvik of each day in hours (`deviation` in `domain/hire.ts`), with a clock where an order waits for an answer, and as rows a line per competence with hours that remain or with orders: per day a thin line of how far it is covered, solid for what is confirmed and hatched for what waits, and the hours missing (−) or over (+). Nothing in Innleid moves the days unless the planner asks to go somewhere: a click on a competence's name opens it on a day that is in view (the first that is short, `openFirst`), and a day is brought into view only when it is outside it (`reveal` in `InnleidScope.tsx`). The arrows beside the days in the editor go to the previous and the next day where hours of the competence are missing (`stepShort`), and «N dager udekket» to the next such day from the days in view on. A click on a day of a competence, a drag across several, or a click on its name picks days, and the label of the competence then is the editor of their orders (`OrderEditor.tsx`): a line per supplier and time with the supplier, the shift, the people, the answer and a cross, and «+ Leverandør», which fills in the people that cover what is missing (`planOrders`). The competence that is open has a line under it for each project, the same lines as in Bemanning, and also each project with orders for it (`projectsWithWork` in `domain/staffing.ts`): the brush and the eraser on it order and empty for that project alone, and a line of the editor has the project its people are for, «Uten prosjekt» by default (`tagOrders`), which the order text names. A day shows a chip per supplier ordered, pale with a clock while it waits; in «Kompakt», the default of the bar, a folded competence is one line of 32 px with one block per day, the chip of its one supplier or a stacked chip for several (`dayBlock`), and a press on the line of coverage of an open competence folds it. An order is «Venter» or «Bekreftet»; a supplier that sends fewer is confirmed with that many, and the rest is missing again (`confirmFewer`). The menus and popovers of the editor (`OrderPopovers.tsx`) close on the first press outside them, which is swallowed. The tools are Velg, Pensel and Tøm (V, B, T; 1 to 9 picks the supplier of the brush, Alt-drag is the eraser for one stroke): the brush orders its supplier across the days drawn over, on those that are short alone, and opens them with the order picked; Tøm removes the orders of the days, every supplier's or the one picked in the bar. What a stroke would do is shown while it is drawn (`StrokePreview`): on a folded competence a badge in outline on the day's own line with the people it would get, so that the line keeps its height under the pointer, on an unfolded one the chip the order would be, with the supplier's name, and for the eraser the days tinted and the chips that would go without their fill. A supplier that does not offer the competence orders nothing. Under the competences are the suppliers (`SupplierRows.tsx`): the people each is ordered for per day, marks for what it offers, and a brush button; with days picked they are those that offer the competence. A click on a supplier's name, or the panel toggle in the page header, slides in the panel of Innleid (`SupplierPanel.tsx`, in the `SidePanel` frame): a supplier's order per day with the answer of each, «Kopier bestilling» (the text of `orderText`), «Alle bekreftet» in one undo step, and what it offers; the matrix of what every supplier offers; and the help, also behind `?`. Esc puts the tool away, then closes the panel, then folds the editor. Keys with days picked: arrows move the days and the line, Shift extends, + and − the people, Enter the answer, Delete removes, Esc folds. What a component of Innleid shows is worked out in `domain/hire.ts` and tested there (the shares of a day's bar, the chips of a day, the name of a shift, the key of a line, a supplier's orders in order); the components only draw it. Innleid reads the balance of `BemanningScope`, whose keys and tools are at rest meanwhile, and holds its own state in `InnleidScope.tsx`: the board (`hireBoard`), the days picked and the edits, each one undo step.
