# Kalender · Bemanning · Innleid

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

Bemanning has two parts on the same days, switched beside the mode («Faste · Innleid», `crewPart`): the permanent staff, described below, and **Innleid** (`ui/innleid/InnleidParts.tsx`), the hours of each competence that remain after them. A day in Innleid is always 116 px wide (`INNLEID_W` in `kalender/zoom.ts`), so that a supplier's name fits beside its people; the days of the permanent staff keep the width their span asks for, and the switch between the parts zooms from the one to the other with the day at the left edge kept (`switchPart` in `Kalender.tsx`). Innleid keeps the date header and the project lines; under them the Avvik of each day in hours, on the line «Mangler / over» (`deviation` in `domain/hire.ts`), with the hatch where an order waits for an answer, and as rows a line per competence with hours that remain or with orders. A day of a competence is Faste's label (design `design_handoff_innleid_v8`, 2026-10-24): the hours that remain as a tint of the competence, filled from the left by what is ordered, confirmed solid first and what waits hatched after, whenever the orders came, and what passes what remains as the ochre tail, hatched while it waits; a rule in the page's colour sets one order from the next, and is dropped where an order is too thin a share to see (`orderedLabel`, drawn by Faste's components in `bemanning/NeedLabel.tsx`, as is the name of a line with its signed hours). On it is what is still missing (−) or over (+), counted against everything ordered (`coverFigure`); a day that is covered has no figure. In «Kompakt», the default of the bar, every line is 32 px and the label is Faste's thin line with the figure beside it, the figure over on an ochre chip. A folded competence names nobody: who covers it is in the tooltip. Nothing in Innleid moves the days unless the planner asks to go somewhere: a click on a competence's name opens it with no days picked (2026-10-24), and a day is brought into view only when it is outside it (`reveal` in `InnleidScope.tsx`); «N dager udekket» goes to the next short day from the days in view on. A click on a day or a drag across several picks days, and the competence opens: its label is the editor of their orders (`OrderEditor.tsx`), and its days show the label on top with a line per supplier and project under it (`coverLines`), what waits hatched and dimmed. Under it is a line for each project, the same lines as in Bemanning, and also each project with orders for it (`projectsWithWork` in `domain/staffing/projectBalance.ts`), and «Uten prosjekt» under them where it has hours or orders in view: what is on none of the projects, worked out as Faste's line (`restDays`), and the orders for none of them (`linesOf` in `InnleidScope.tsx`). In «Detaljert» a project's line is 46 px, with a small label at the top and under it the supplier with the most confirmed, its people, and one «+N» for the others, hatched where one of them waits (`coveredBy`). As in Faste, the lines under it are neutral, the line the days are picked on has the mark at its name (the editor's edge where it is the competence's own), and the other competences step back and shrink to their thin line. Days picked on a project's line are picked for it: the editor lists that line's orders and names it in its head, and a click on the line's name picks the same days on it, or back on the competence. The editor is slim: a head with the fold button and what the days are, and a line of 32 px per supplier, time and project, segments on a sunken track with the one picked raised; each has the supplier (with the project in small type on the competence's own, the time where it is not «Dag», and «2/3 d» where it lacks days), the people, the answer, which opens the answer popover, and ⋯: fill the days it lacks (`fillGroup`), another supplier, another shift or time, move it to another line (`tagOrders`), or remove it. Under them is the supplier of the brush, picked from those that offer the competence: picking it orders nothing, it takes the brush (2026-10-24, the planner's word: choosing a supplier is not painting). An order is «Venter» or «Bekreftet»; a supplier that sends fewer is confirmed with that many, and the rest is missing again (`confirmFewer`). The menus and popovers of the editor (`OrderPopovers.tsx`) close on the first press outside them, which is swallowed. The tools are Velg, Pensel and Tøm (V, B, T; 1 to 9 picks the supplier of the brush, Alt-drag is the eraser for one stroke): the brush orders its supplier across the days drawn over: on the competence's own line what each project and «Uten prosjekt» lacks, each order tagged with its project, or on a line under it for that line alone; where no day lacks anything, one person a day for the first line with hours (`planAcross`); and it opens the days with the order picked. With a competence open, the supplier list of the bar has those that offer it first, and the others cannot be picked; Tøm removes the orders of the days, of the line it is drawn on, every supplier's unless one is picked for it in the bar, apart from the brush's supplier (2026-10-24). What a stroke would do is shown while it is drawn (`StrokePreview`): the days it covers are tinted, and their labels show what they would be after it. A supplier that does not offer the competence orders nothing. Under the competences are the suppliers (`SupplierRows.tsx`): the people each is ordered for per day, those that wait hatched, with a rule under each line and none between the days, marks for what it offers, and a brush button; with days picked they are those that offer the competence. The panel toggle in the page header, and «N t venter på svar» in the bar, slide in the control panel of the suppliers (`SupplierPanel.tsx`, in the `SidePanel` frame, 2026-10-24): every supplier with orders, those that wait soonest first (`supplierReview`), each with what waits and what is confirmed, its order as text to copy (`orderText`), «Bekreft alle» in one undo step, and its orders by day with the answer of each, which a click turns; «Venter», the default, lists only what waits, «Alle» everything. A click on an order shows it in the grid, and a click on a supplier's name, there or on its row, opens its own panel: its order per day, what it is ordered for in all, and what it offers. The help is behind `?`. What every supplier offers is set on Mannskap › Leverandører; the panel's matrix of it is gone. Esc puts the tool away, then closes the panel, then folds the editor. Keys with days picked: ← → move the days, Shift extends, ↑ ↓ pick the line, + and − the people, Enter the answer, Delete removes, Esc folds. What a component of Innleid shows is worked out in `domain/hire.ts` and tested there (the pieces of a day's label, who covers a project's day, the lines of an open day, the orders shared out on the lines, the name of a shift, the key of a line, a supplier's orders in order); the components only draw it. Innleid reads the balance of `BemanningScope`, whose keys and tools are at rest meanwhile, and holds its own state in `InnleidScope.tsx`: the board (`hireBoard`), the days picked and the edits, each one undo step.
