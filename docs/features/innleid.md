# Kalender · Bemanning · Innleid

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

Bemanning has two parts on the same days, switched beside the mode («Faste · Innleid», `crewPart`): the permanent staff, described below, and **Innleid** (`ui/innleid/InnleidParts.tsx`), the hours of each competence that remain after them. A day in Innleid is always 116 px wide (`INNLEID_W` in `kalender/zoom.ts`), so that a supplier's name fits beside its people; the days of the permanent staff keep the width their span asks for, and the switch between the parts zooms from the one to the other with the day at the left edge kept (`switchPart` in `Kalender.tsx`). Innleid keeps the date header and the project lines; under them the Avvik of each day in hours, on the line «Mangler / over» (`deviation` in `domain/hire.ts`): what is missing after the permanent staff and the confirmed orders, per competence, so it shrinks as the suppliers answer; an order that waits counts nowhere and gives the day the hatch, and the line is the same in Faste (`bemanning/AvvikSection.tsx`, 2026-10-25), and as rows a line per competence with hours that remain or with orders. A day of a competence is Faste's label (design `design_handoff_innleid_v8`, 2026-10-24): the hours that remain as a tint of the competence, filled from the left by what is ordered, confirmed solid first and what waits hatched after, whenever the orders came, and what passes what remains as the ochre tail, hatched while it waits; a rule in the page's colour sets one order from the next, and is dropped where an order is too thin a share to see (`orderedLabel`, drawn by Faste's components in `bemanning/NeedLabel.tsx`, as is the name of a line with its signed hours). On it are the hours ordered, waiting or not, of those that remain («7,5/15», `of`, 2026-10-26), as on Faste's labels, and where the label is too narrow for them, what is still missing (−) or over (+), counted against everything ordered (`coverFigure`). In «Kompakt», the default, switched by the icon at «Kompetanse» in the heading as Faste's sections are (`DensityToggle`, 2026-10-26), a line is 14 px and its label Faste's thin line, 6 px thick, with the signed figure beside it in ink, on no fill (the ochre tail and the red hatch of the line say over and short), as in Faste; otherwise a line is 32 px, Faste's heights (2026-10-26). A folded competence names nobody: who covers it is in the tooltip. Nothing in Innleid moves the days unless the planner asks to go somewhere: a click on a competence's name opens it with no days picked (2026-10-24), and a day is brought into view only when it is outside it (`reveal` in `InnleidScope.tsx`); «N dager udekket» counts, and goes to, the days short of what is ordered, waiting or not, from the days in view on, so it does not lead back to days already ordered. A press is followed wherever the pointer goes, and the grid scrolls on under a still pointer near its edges, by Faste's hook (`bemanning/useFollowPointer.ts`, 2026-10-26). Under each competence is a line for each project, the same lines as in Bemanning, and also each project with orders for it (`projectsWithWork` in `domain/staffing/projectBalance.ts`), and «Uten prosjekt» under them where it has hours or orders in view: what is on none of the projects, worked out as Faste's line (`restDays`); the orders for no project are shared on the project lines a whole person at a time to the line that lacks most, confirmed before waiting (`shareOrders`). The competence (and a project's line under it) is the brush, as in Faste, and follows between Faste and Innleid (2026-10-26): a click on a competence's name or on one of its days makes it the brush, a click on a project's line under it the project, and a click on the brush's sum line again puts it away; 1 to 9 picks the competence, ↑ ↓ step between the sum line and its project lines. Between the demand and the suppliers is the bar, where Faste has it (2026-10-26, slice 3): the tools Velg, Pensel and Tøm (V, B, T; Alt-drag is Tøm for one stroke), the brush with its swatch, competence and project, and its count of people, «− 2 + pers.», stepped by the buttons or + and −, and kept per competence in the browser (`innleidCounts`). Pensel with no brush takes the competence with the most missing in the days in view. The brush paints on the suppliers' shift lines (`paintOrders`): its count of people on every line and day the stroke is drawn over, waiting, for the project of the brush or for none from the sum line, and on a line and day that has that competence and project already the count replaces what is there (I7); a supplier that does not offer the competence is skipped, and a stroke where none of the lines offers it says so. With Shift the stroke goes no further than the last day the brush's line lacks hours on. Tøm removes the orders of the days and lines drawn over (`clearOrders`); a stroke started on a run removes only that competence and project (its lane, `data-lane`), with Shift, from an empty day, or with Alt everything (the designer's rule, the planner's choice). While a stroke is drawn, it is framed with Faste's outline and pill (`StrokeBox`: the brush, «N dager × M linjer», the people, how many lines are skipped), the runs it adds or changes are drawn as they would be and those it removes are marked, and the demand above shows what it would be after it. With Velg a press selects days on the lines, framed with «N dager × M linjer · Del fjerner · Esc», Delete removes their orders, and Cmd+C and Cmd+V copy them and paste them from the line and day under the pointer, or from the first day selected (`copyOrders`, `pasteOrders`), with a message of what was pasted and skipped (slice 4, 2026-10-26). A drag from a run with Velg moves the day's orders on its line to another day or line, and Alt copies them (`moveOrders`, as Faste's `moveDay`); a supplier that does not offer the competence keeps them where they were. An end of a run is pulled with any tool, by the grip at its end (`.bm-edge`, as in Faste): outward it orders the run's count on the working days it goes over, waiting, inward it takes days away, and with Shift it goes no further than the last day the competence lacks hours on (`stretchOrders`, over days off as `grips` of `orderRuns`). A click on a run selects its day and opens its popover (`OrderPopover` in `OrderPopovers.tsx`), for the whole run, or, inside days selected before, for the orders of all of them: the answer (Venter, Bekreftet, and «Stiller N av M» for fewer), the people, the times with the shift's back, the project and «Fjern», each one undo step, and «Bare denne dagen» for the day clicked alone. There is no menu on a right-click: the popover does what Faste's menu does. A click on a day of the demand makes its competence, or the project's line, the brush (with Velg kept until «Bestill» is pressed) and opens the day's balance, the popover Faste opens there (`DemandPopover` in `bemanning/Popovers.tsx`, shared since 2026-10-26): after Faste, confirmed, waiting and missing or over, and the orders of the day; Faste's has the free time and moved hours under its figures (`CarryWork`). Innleid's messages are drawn by `InnleidOverlays.tsx`. A supplier with no shift has a link «Ingen skift» to Leverandører. An order is «Venter» or «Bekreftet»; a supplier that sends fewer is confirmed with that many, and the rest is missing again (`confirmFewer`). New times in the popover are a draft until «Sett tider» or Enter, so a week of own times for a run or the days selected is one change and one undo step; the orders stay on their shift line (slice 5, 2026-10-26). Under the competences are the suppliers (`SupplierRows.tsx`), as the people under them in Faste (design `design_handoff_innleid_v9`, 2026-10-26): a line per shift the supplier offers (`supplierLines`), as high as a person's, with its orders as runs over the days (`orderRuns`): one piece in the competence's colour with ink on it, hatched in the colour while it waits, named «Snekker · Hage 3» at the left edge of the days, «2/3» where fewer were confirmed than asked and «10–18» where the times are not the shift's. Each competence and project ordered on a line in the days drawn has a lane in height, in the order of Kompetanser, so a run keeps its lane from day to day: one fills the line, two share it, and from three the line grows by 16 px a lane (`shiftLine` in `bemanning/layout.ts`). A supplier with more than one shift has a name line over them with the people per day over its shifts, those that wait hatched (`supplierDays`), which folds them, remembered per browser; one with a single shift has its name on that line. The name has marks for what it offers; a click on it opens the supplier's panel. A shift the supplier no longer offers keeps its line while it has orders, struck through with «Tilbys ikke lenger» and the mark of attention, and a run of a competence it no longer offers has the mark before its name. With a competence open, the suppliers that offer it come first, and the others are dimmed under a divider, as people without it in Faste. The panel toggle in the page header, and «N t venter på svar» in the bar, slide in the control panel of the suppliers (`SupplierPanel.tsx`, in the `SidePanel` frame, 2026-10-24): every supplier with orders, those that wait soonest first (`supplierReview`), each with its name on a line of its own and under it what waits and what is confirmed, its order as text to copy (`orderText`, with the shift and times of each line), «Bekreft alle» in one undo step, and its orders by day with the answer of each, which a click turns; «Venter», the default, lists only what waits, «Alle» everything. A click on an order shows it in the grid, and a click on a supplier's name, there or on its row, opens its own panel: its order per day, what it is ordered for in all, and what it offers. The help is behind `?`. What every supplier offers is set on Mannskap › Leverandører; the panel's matrix of it is gone. The keys are Faste's (`bemanning/useBemanningKeys.ts`, 2026-10-26): Esc stops a stroke, then lets go of the days selected, then puts the brush away, then closes the panel; + − step the brush's count; ↑ ↓ step between the brush's lines; Delete removes the orders of the popover open, else of the days selected; Cmd+C and Cmd+V copy and paste. What a component of Innleid shows is worked out in `domain/hire.ts` and tested there (the pieces of a day's label, the orders painted, emptied and shared out on the lines, the runs in lanes, a supplier's people per day, the name of a shift, the key of a line); the components only draw it. Innleid reads the balance of `BemanningScope`, whose keys and tools are at rest meanwhile, and holds its own state in `InnleidScope.tsx`: the board (`hireBoard`), the brush and its count, the stroke and the days selected (through Faste's `bemanning/useStrokeInput.ts`, which takes the lines of either part), and the edits, each one undo step.
