# Kalender · Bemanning · Innleid

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

Bemanning has two parts on the same days, switched beside the mode («Faste · Innleid», `crewPart`): the permanent staff, described below, and **Innleid** (`ui/innleid/InnleidParts.tsx`), the hours of each competence that remain after them. A day in Innleid is always 116 px wide (`INNLEID_W` in `kalender/zoom.ts`), so that a supplier's name fits beside its people; the days of the permanent staff keep the width their span asks for, and the switch between the parts zooms from the one to the other with the day at the left edge kept (`switchPart` in `Kalender.tsx`). Innleid keeps the date header and the project lines; under them the Avvik of each day in hours, on the line «Mangler / over» (`deviation` in `domain/hire.ts`): what is missing after the permanent staff and the confirmed orders, per competence, so it shrinks as the suppliers answer; an order that waits counts nowhere and gives the day the hatch, and the line is the same in Faste (`bemanning/AvvikSection.tsx`, 2026-10-25), and as rows a line per competence with hours that remain or with orders. A day of a competence is Faste's label (design `design_handoff_innleid_v8`, 2026-10-24): the hours that remain as a tint of the competence, filled from the left by what is ordered, confirmed solid first and what waits hatched after, whenever the orders came, and what passes what remains as the ochre tail, hatched while it waits; a rule in the page's colour sets one order from the next, and is dropped where an order is too thin a share to see (`orderedLabel`, drawn by Faste's components in `bemanning/NeedLabel.tsx`, as is the name of a line with its signed hours). On it are the hours ordered, waiting or not, of those that remain («7,5/15», `of`, 2026-10-26), as on Faste's labels, and where the label is too narrow for them, what is still missing (−) or over (+), counted against everything ordered (`coverFigure`). In «Kompakt», the default, switched by the icon at «Kompetanse» in the heading as Faste's sections are (`DensityToggle`, 2026-10-26), a line is 14 px and its label Faste's thin line, 6 px thick, with the signed figure beside it in ink, on no fill (the ochre tail and the red hatch of the line say over and short), as in Faste; otherwise a line is 32 px, Faste's heights (2026-10-26). A folded competence names nobody: who covers it is in the tooltip. Nothing in Innleid moves the days unless the planner asks to go somewhere: a click on a competence's name opens it with no days picked (2026-10-24), and a day is brought into view only when it is outside it (`reveal` in `InnleidScope.tsx`); «N dager udekket» counts, and goes to, the days short of what is ordered, waiting or not, from the days in view on, so it does not lead back to days already ordered. A click on a day or a drag across several picks days, and the competence opens; a click on the one day picked lets go of it. A press is followed wherever the pointer goes, and the grid scrolls on under a still pointer near its edges, by Faste's hook (`bemanning/useFollowPointer.ts`, 2026-10-26). When days are picked, its label is the editor of their orders (`OrderEditor.tsx`), and its days show the label on top with a line per supplier and project under it (`coverLines`), what waits hatched and dimmed. Under it is a line for each project, the same lines as in Bemanning, and also each project with orders for it (`projectsWithWork` in `domain/staffing/projectBalance.ts`), and «Uten prosjekt» under them where it has hours or orders in view: what is on none of the projects, worked out as Faste's line (`restDays`), and the orders for none of them (`linesOf` in `InnleidScope.tsx`). In «Detaljert» a project's line is 46 px, with a small label at the top and under it the supplier with the most confirmed, its people, and one «+N» for the others, hatched where one of them waits (`coveredBy`). As in Faste, the lines under it are neutral, the line the days are picked on has the mark at its name (the editor's edge where it is the competence's own) and a faint tint of the competence over its days (2026-10-26), and the other competences step back and shrink to their thin line. Days picked on a project's line are picked for it: the editor lists that line's orders and names it in its head, and a click on the line's name picks the same days on it, or back on the competence. The editor is slim: a head with the fold button and what the days are, and a line of 32 px per supplier, time and project, segments on a sunken track with the one picked raised; each has the supplier (with the project in small type on the competence's own, the time where it is not «Dag», and «2/3 d» where it lacks days), the people, the answer, which opens the answer popover, and ⋯: fill the days it lacks (`fillGroup`), another supplier, another shift or time, move it to another line (`tagOrders`), or remove it. Under them is the supplier of the brush, picked from those that offer the competence: picking it orders nothing, it takes the brush (2026-10-24, the planner's word: choosing a supplier is not painting), and picking it again, there or with the brush button of its row, puts the brush away: no supplier, and Velg (`dropBrush`, 2026-10-26). A day under the pointer has no tint of its own. An order is «Venter» or «Bekreftet»; a supplier that sends fewer is confirmed with that many, and the rest is missing again (`confirmFewer`). The menus and popovers of the editor (`OrderPopovers.tsx`) close on the first press outside them, which is swallowed. The tools are Velg, Pensel and Tøm (V, B, T; 1 to 9 picks the supplier of the brush, Alt-drag is the eraser for one stroke): the brush orders its supplier across the days drawn over: on the competence's own line what each project and «Uten prosjekt» lacks, each order tagged with its project, or on a line under it for that line alone; where no day lacks anything, one person a day for the first line with hours (`planAcross`); and it opens the days with the order picked. With a competence open, the supplier list of the bar has those that offer it first, and the others cannot be picked; Tøm removes the orders of the days, of the line it is drawn on, every supplier's unless one is picked for it in the bar, apart from the brush's supplier (2026-10-24). What a stroke would do is shown while it is drawn (`StrokePreview`): the days it covers are framed with Faste's outline and pill (`StrokeBox`: the supplier, the days, the hours and the people the brush would order, «Tøm» for the eraser), and their labels show what they would be after it; the days picked keep the frame, with «N dager · Esc fjern» (2026-10-26). A supplier that does not offer the competence orders nothing. Under the competences are the suppliers (`SupplierRows.tsx`), as the people under them in Faste (design `design_handoff_innleid_v9`, 2026-10-26): a line per shift the supplier offers (`supplierLines`), as high as a person's, with its orders as runs over the days (`orderRuns`): one piece in the competence's colour with ink on it, hatched in the colour while it waits, named «Snekker · Hage 3» at the left edge of the days, «2/3» where fewer were confirmed than asked and «10–18» where the times are not the shift's. Each competence and project ordered on a line in the days drawn has a lane in height, in the order of Kompetanser, so a run keeps its lane from day to day: one fills the line, two share it, and from three the line grows by 16 px a lane (`shiftLine` in `bemanning/layout.ts`). A supplier with more than one shift has a name line over them with the people per day over its shifts, those that wait hatched (`supplierDays`), which folds them, remembered per browser; one with a single shift has its name on that line. The name has marks for what it offers and a brush button. A shift the supplier no longer offers keeps its line while it has orders, struck through with «Tilbys ikke lenger» and the mark of attention, and a run of a competence it no longer offers has the mark before its name. With a competence open, the suppliers that offer it come first, and the others are dimmed under a divider, as people without it in Faste. The lines only show the orders so far: they are painted from slice 3 of the build plan. The panel toggle in the page header, and «N t venter på svar» in the bar, slide in the control panel of the suppliers (`SupplierPanel.tsx`, in the `SidePanel` frame, 2026-10-24): every supplier with orders, those that wait soonest first (`supplierReview`), each with its name on a line of its own and under it what waits and what is confirmed, its order as text to copy (`orderText`), «Bekreft alle» in one undo step, and its orders by day with the answer of each, which a click turns; «Venter», the default, lists only what waits, «Alle» everything. A click on an order shows it in the grid, and a click on a supplier's name, there or on its row, opens its own panel: its order per day, what it is ordered for in all, and what it offers. The help is behind `?`. What every supplier offers is set on Mannskap › Leverandører; the panel's matrix of it is gone. The keys are Faste's (`bemanning/useBemanningKeys.ts`, 2026-10-26): Esc stops a stroke, then lets go of the days picked, then puts the brush away, then closes the panel, then folds the competence; ↑ ↓ step between the lines under the open competence, keeping the days; Delete removes the order line picked in the editor, else the orders of the days picked on their line. The arrows no longer move the days, and + −, Enter and Shift-click are gone. What a component of Innleid shows is worked out in `domain/hire.ts` and tested there (the pieces of a day's label, who covers a project's day, the lines of an open day, the orders shared out on the lines, the name of a shift, the key of a line, a supplier's orders in order); the components only draw it. Innleid reads the balance of `BemanningScope`, whose keys and tools are at rest meanwhile, and holds its own state in `InnleidScope.tsx`: the board (`hireBoard`), the days picked and the edits, each one undo step.
