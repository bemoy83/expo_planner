# Kalender · Bemanning · Innleid

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

Bemanning has two parts on the same days, switched beside the mode («Faste · Innleid», `crewPart`): the permanent staff, described below, and **Innleid** (`ui/innleid/InnleidParts.tsx`), the hours of each competence that remain after them. The days of Innleid are half again as wide as those of the permanent staff, whose width is the one their span asks for and the one kept (`partWidth` in `kalender/zoom.ts`, 2026-10-27; the same width from 2026-10-26, and 116 px of its own before): the room for the marks and names on a supplier's day, and the switch between the parts zooms as the switch between the modes does, into Innleid before its rows come, out of it after Faste's, with the day in focus kept where it is on screen where it is in view, else the middle of the view (`switchPart`, `zoomAnchor`); «Tilpass prosjekt» fits the days at the width of the part shown, and the days remembered for Bemanning are as many as Faste shows. Innleid keeps the date header and the project lines; under them the demand per competence, and last, right over the planning bar as in Plan (2026-10-27), the Avvik of each day in hours, on the line «Mangler / over» (`deviation` in `domain/hire.ts`): what is missing after the permanent staff and the confirmed orders, per competence, so it shrinks as the suppliers answer; an order that waits counts nowhere and gives the day the hatch, and the line is the same in Faste (`bemanning/AvvikSection.tsx`, 2026-10-25), and as rows Faste's list of competences (`CompetenceList` in `bemanning/DemandRows.tsx`, 2026-10-27): the same competences in the same order, with the same keys 1 to 9, folded to the one in focus by the same twisty; those with nothing left after the permanent staff and no orders lie under «Uten behov», and one no supplier offers is greyed, its key picking nothing. A day of a competence is Faste's label (design `design_handoff_innleid_v8`, 2026-10-24): the hours that remain as a tint of the competence, filled from the left by what is ordered, confirmed solid first and what waits hatched after, whenever the orders came, and what passes what remains as the ochre tail, hatched while it waits; a rule in the page's colour sets one order from the next, and is dropped where an order is too thin a share to see (`orderedLabel`, drawn by Faste's components in `bemanning/NeedLabel.tsx`: the line is Faste's `NeedRow`, with its rule under it and none between the days, 2026-10-26, and so is the name of a line with its signed hours). On it are the people ordered, waiting or not, of those the day needs («2/3», `of`), and where the label is too narrow for them, the people still missing (−) or over (+) (`coverFigure`): Innleid counts in whole people (2026-10-27), the hours that remain divided by a normal day («Dag», `personDay`) and rounded up (`Coverage.need`), with the hours in the tooltip; the fill of the label is hours. The label column says the people missing over the days in view («−3 pers.», `peopleMissingIn`), and «Mangler / over», shared with Faste, stays in hours. In «Kompakt», switched by the icon at «Kompetanse» in the heading, one setting for Faste and Innleid (`demandDensity`, 2026-10-27), and never for the competence in focus and the lines under it, which are always drawn in full, a line is 14 px and its label Faste's thin line, 6 px thick, with the signed figure beside it in ink, on no fill (the ochre tail and the red hatch of the line say over and short), as in Faste; otherwise a line is 32 px, Faste's heights (2026-10-26). A folded competence names nobody: who covers it is in the tooltip. Nothing in Innleid moves the days unless the planner asks to go somewhere: a click on a competence's name opens it with no days picked (2026-10-24), and a day is brought into view only when it is outside it (`reveal` in `InnleidScope.tsx`); «N dager udekket» counts, and goes to, the days short of what is ordered, waiting or not, from the days in view on, so it does not lead back to days already ordered. A press is followed wherever the pointer goes, and the grid scrolls on under a still pointer near its edges, by Faste's hook (`bemanning/useFollowPointer.ts`, 2026-10-26). Under each competence is a line for each project, the same lines as in Bemanning, and also each project with orders for it (`projectsWithWork` in `domain/staffing/projectBalance.ts`), and «Uten prosjekt» under them where it has hours or orders in view: what is on none of the projects, worked out as Faste's line (`restDays`); the orders for no project are shared on the project lines a whole person at a time to the line that lacks most, confirmed before waiting (`shareOrders`). The competence (and a project's line under it) is the brush, as in Faste, and follows between Faste and Innleid (2026-10-26): a click on a competence's name or on one of its days makes it the brush, a click on a project's line under it the project, and a click on the brush's sum line again puts it away; 1 to 9 picks the competence, ↑ ↓ step between the sum line and its project lines. With project lines under it the competence's line shows no figures in its days, as an open level of the plan (2026-10-27). The header, the bar and the heading over the suppliers are Faste's frame with Innleid's contents (`bemanning/CrewFrame.tsx`, 2026-10-26), and the bar has Faste's project, which dims the others, and «Tilpass prosjekt», which fits the days to it. Between the demand and the suppliers is the bar, where Faste has it (2026-10-26, slice 3): the tools Velg, Pensel and Tøm (V, B, T; Alt-drag is Tøm for one stroke), the brush with its swatch, competence and project, and its count of people, «− 2 + pers.», stepped by the buttons or + and −, and kept per competence in the browser (`innleidCounts`); under 1 it is «Auto» (2026-10-27): each day painted gets the whole people of the line's shift that the brush's line still lacks there, counted against every other order of its competence and project, waiting or not, and the lines before it in the stroke, and a day that lacks none gets nothing (`Paint.count` in `paintOrders`). Pensel with no brush takes the competence with the most missing in the days in view. The brush paints on the suppliers' shift lines (`paintOrders`): its count of people on every line and day the stroke is drawn over, waiting, for the project of the brush or for none from the sum line, and on a line and day that has that competence and project already the count replaces what is there (I7); a supplier that does not offer the competence is skipped, and a stroke where none of the lines offers it says so. With Shift the stroke goes no further than the last day the brush's line lacks hours on. Tøm removes the orders of the days and lines drawn over (`clearOrders`); a stroke started on a run removes only that competence and project (its lane, `data-lane`), with Shift, from an empty day, or with Alt everything (the designer's rule, the planner's choice). While a stroke is drawn, it is framed with Faste's outline and pill (`StrokeBox`: the brush, «N dager × M linjer», the people, the hours it orders or takes away, `hoursChange`, and how many lines are skipped), the runs it adds or changes are drawn as they would be and those it removes are marked, and the demand above shows what it would be after it. Under a still pointer the brush shows what a click would order on that day and line, on the lines alone (2026-10-26). The tool and the brush are Faste's (`bemanning/useBrushPick.ts`, which `useBrush` puts its lines and places around). With Velg a press selects days on the lines, framed with «N dager × M linjer · Del fjerner · Esc», Delete removes their orders, and Cmd+C and Cmd+V copy them and paste them from the line and day under the pointer, or from the first day selected (`copyOrders`, `pasteOrders`), with a message of what was pasted and skipped (slice 4, 2026-10-26). A drag from a run with Velg moves the day's orders on its line to another day or line, and Alt copies them (`moveOrders`, as Faste's `moveDay`); a supplier that does not offer the competence keeps them where they were. An end of a run is pulled with any tool, by the grip at its end (`.bm-edge`, as in Faste): outward it orders the run's count on the working days it goes over, waiting, inward it takes days away, and with Shift it goes no further than the last day the competence lacks hours on (`stretchOrders`, over days off as `grips` of `orderRuns`). A click on a run selects its day and opens its popover (`OrderPopover` in `OrderPopovers.tsx`), for the whole run, or, inside days selected before, for the orders of all of them: the answer (Venter, Bekreftet, and «Stiller N av M» for fewer), the people, the times with the shift's back, the project and «Fjern», each one undo step, and «Bare denne dagen» for the day clicked alone. There is no menu on a right-click: the popover does what Faste's menu does. A click on a day of the demand makes its competence, or the project's line, the brush (with Velg kept until «Bestill» is pressed) and opens the day's balance, the popover Faste opens there (`DemandPopover` in `bemanning/Popovers.tsx`, shared since 2026-10-26): after Faste, confirmed, waiting and missing or over, and the orders of the day; Faste's has the free time and moved hours under its figures (`CarryWork`). Innleid's messages are drawn by `InnleidOverlays.tsx`. A supplier with no shift has a link «Ingen skift» to Leverandører. An order is «Venter» or «Bekreftet»; a supplier that sends fewer is confirmed with that many, and the rest is missing again (`confirmFewer`). New times in the popover are a draft until «Sett tider» or Enter, so a week of own times for a run or the days selected is one change and one undo step; the orders stay on their shift line (slice 5, 2026-10-26). Under the competences are the suppliers (`SupplierRows.tsx`), as the people under them in Faste (design `design_handoff_innleid_v9`, 2026-10-26): a line per shift the supplier offers (`supplierLines`), as high as a person's, with its orders as runs over the days (`orderRuns`): one piece in the competence's colour with ink on it, hatched in the colour while it waits, named «Snekker · Hage 3» at the left edge of the days, «2/3» where fewer were confirmed than asked and «10–18» where the times are not the shift's. Each competence and project ordered on a line in the days drawn has a lane in height, in the order of Kompetanser, so a run keeps its lane from day to day: one fills the line, two share it, and from three the line grows by 16 px a lane (`shiftLine` in `bemanning/layout.ts`). Every supplier has a name line over its shifts, one or several (2026-10-27), as high as a person's, with per day a bar in the colours of the competences ordered over its shifts, each as long as its people, those that wait hatched as a run that waits, as tall as a painted block of Faste and named on it: in full for one competence, short for two, by colour alone for more (`partName`), in the order of Kompetanser, and the people in all beside it (`supplierDays`, 2026-10-27), and the mark of attention before a day that waits for an answer and is today or among the next two days (`isUrgent`, `URGENT_DAYS`), as after the supplier's name and in «N t venter på svar» in the bar (`urgentWaits`): who to ask again; the name line folds them, remembered per browser; the shifts are indented a step further than the name. There is no strong line between the suppliers, as none between the people. The name is a person's in Faste (`CrewName` in `bemanning/CrewFrame.tsx`, 2026-10-27): the name, with the mark of orders that wait after it, and under it a mark per competence of the list above, in its order, filled for what it offers; every name stands where the arrow leaves it, with or without one. A click on it opens the supplier's panel. At the right is the week of the people's in Faste: the hours ordered from it in that week, waiting or not, with no bar, since a supplier has no capacity («uke · timer»). A shift the supplier no longer offers keeps its line while it has orders, struck through with «Tilbys ikke lenger» and the mark of attention, and a run of a competence it no longer offers has the mark before its name. With a competence open, the suppliers that offer it come first, and the others are dimmed under a divider, as people without it in Faste. The panel toggle in the page header, and «N t venter på svar» in the bar, slide in the control panel of the suppliers (`SupplierPanel.tsx`, in the `SidePanel` frame, 2026-10-24): every supplier with orders, those that wait soonest first (`supplierReview`), each with its name on a line of its own and under it what waits and what is confirmed, its order as text to copy (`orderText`, with the shift and times of each line), «Bekreft alle» in one undo step, and its orders by day with the answer of each, which a click turns; «Venter», the default, lists only what waits, «Alle» everything. A click on an order shows it in the grid, and a click on a supplier's name, there or on its row, opens its own panel: its order per day, what it is ordered for in all, and what it offers. The help is behind `?`. What every supplier offers is set on Mannskap › Leverandører; the panel's matrix of it is gone. The keys are Faste's (`bemanning/useBemanningKeys.ts`, 2026-10-26): Esc stops a stroke, then lets go of the days selected, then puts the brush away, then closes the panel; + − step the brush's count; ↑ ↓ step between the brush's lines; Delete removes the orders of the popover open, else of the days selected; Cmd+C and Cmd+V copy and paste. What a component of Innleid shows is worked out in `domain/hire.ts` and tested there (the pieces of a day's label, the orders painted, emptied and shared out on the lines, the runs in lanes, a supplier's people per day, the name of a shift, the key of a line); the components only draw it. Innleid reads the balance of `BemanningScope`, whose keys and tools are at rest meanwhile, and holds its own state in `InnleidScope.tsx`: the board (`hireBoard`), the brush and its count, the stroke and the days selected (through Faste's `bemanning/useStrokeInput.ts`, which takes the lines of either part), and the edits, each one undo step.

**One person on several competences** (slice 6, 2026-10-28; the Claude Doc «Innleid skive 6: én person på flere kompetanser», https://claude.ai/artifact/9wE4byCu9GkwmYYeVGP6BG, and its review, https://claude.ai/artifact/38qYHhVXLhVGtg5m2SjA6c). Where a supplier's day lacks a little of two competences, one hired person can do both. The supplier's panel lists the days of its lines where its orders could be fewer people as shared ones, «2 FOGA + 1 Elektro kan bli 2 personer», each with «Slå sammen» (`sharingOffers` and `sharePerson` in `domain/hire.ts`): an order per competence and project stays, each with its share of the person as `fte`, joined by `HireOrder.person`, and the shares add up to whole people. They are shared as each lane lacks hours without the orders selected, by the rule Auto of the brush uses (`lackOf`), unrounded; it is offered only where every lane lacks hours and it makes fewer people. It is a new question, so it waits. A shared person is answered, counted, timed, moved, copied, pulled and removed whole; one that comes apart is mended after every change (`normalizePersons`): a part alone is an order of its own, its people rounded up. On the supplier's line each part stands in the lane of its competence and project as any order, its number the hours of its share with a link before the name (`orderRuns`: `shared`); where a lane holds an order of its own on the day, the part gets a second lane, kept from day to day while the run goes on. A part's project is its own. A click on a part opens the popover for the whole person, with what each person does. The shared people are listed in the supplier's panel too, each with «Del opp», which makes the parts orders of their own again, rounded up, waiting. The brush leaves a shared person alone and paints beside it what is still missing; Tøm in the lane of a part takes the whole person. The order text has one line per shared person with the hours of each part. Innleid counts a day's people from the hours (`coverage`, 2026-10-28): the people missing are the hours missing in whole people of a normal day, rounded up, and those over the hours over, rounded down, so a share covers what its hours cover, and an order of shorter times than the day shift counts as less than a person. «Til innleie» of the planning engine lists a person who goes on to another competence under the one hired for, with «også» the other (`HireNeed.also`); the engine's drafts are never stored and are not marked as shared.
