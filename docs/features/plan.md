# Kalender · Plan

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

**Kalender** – the planning workspace. Date header, hall calendar and staffing totals are pinned above the planning rows, and right above the rows sits the planning bar with the tools (`kalender/PlanBar.tsx`): Velg, Fordel behov and Tøm (keys V, B, T, as in Bemanning; Alt-drag gives the eraser for one stroke, and with Velg Shift extends the selection, on a click and along a drag), filter, grouping, the level of detail (1, 2, 3 … by the levels of «Grupper etter», or «Alle»: every branch folded to that level, what was folded by hand let go, `shownLevels` and `foldedTo` in `kalender/rows.ts`; a chevron still folds one branch) and zoom. «Steder» in the page header (`kalender/AreaMenu.tsx`) is a tree of the planner's areas with their halls, to tick the ones to work with, and under it the statuses of Venyou whose bookings count, in both modes: the hall calendar shows the ticked halls and the bookings of the ticked statuses, a project booked in unticked halls or with unticked statuses alone is left out of the rows and of Bemanning's projects, and its FTE out of the need and the demand. The hall calendar has a compact view, as the project lines of Bemanning have (`DensityToggle`): a thin bar per phase without letters or names. What is unticked is kept per browser (`HallFilter` in `domain/areas.ts`). The day under the pointer, over the dates, the top block or the rows, is traced down the date header and the top block, not the rows, and the planning row under it across, both in the one ink `--trace`, a step stronger than a weekend (`dayHover` in `Kalender.tsx`, set by a style variable so no line is drawn again). In Bemanning and Innleid the day is traced the same way, down the date header, the project lines and the demand, from the pointer over them or over the people and the competences. A right-click on a planning cell opens a menu for the cell and its row, and the panel toggle in the page header slides row details in over the grid (`kalender/RowInspector.tsx`). The Avvik line is a heat map of three answers (`kalender/heat.ts`): red where crew is missing, darker the more; one flat green where it is ok, up to one FTE to spare; ochre where more is over. Its figures are signed, and a zero is not written. It is switched off under Innstillinger. The rows form a hierarchy the planner arranges («Grupper etter»: Prosjekt and Arbeidsfase first, locked (`FIXED_LEVELS`), then Hall/Sted, Kompetanse and Avd. in any order); FTE per day is stored on the rows and every level above sums. A folded level below a project is the deepest line of its branch and takes numbers itself, with no switch to turn on; an open one only sums, and so does a folded project, on its hall-phase strip, since FTE is shared out within a work phase. A number typed on a folded level is shared out by required hours over its rows that can be worked on that day, those whose window holds it or that have no window, and over all of them where none can (`spreadOpen` in `domain/spread.ts`). The days in a row a planning row has FTE are a span, and its first and last cell are also its grips, with any tool: a press there dragged sideways pulls that end along the days (`stretchSpan` in `domain/spread.ts`, `pullPreview` in `kalender/strokes.ts`), while a click still selects the cell and a drag up or down selects or draws as from any cell. Outward the new working days get the span's crew, inward the days let go of are cleared and the day at the other end stays; an end stops on the day before the row's next planned day. Shift held while it is pulled stops the end where the row's demand is met, from the press or from later on, and Alt pressed once the pull has started keeps the sum and shares it again. Alt held at the press is the eraser on an end cell as on any other, and a Shift-click on one, or a Shift-drag up or down from it, is what it is on any cell. The cells, the Avvik line and the row's Planlagt and Δ show what the pull would leave while it lasts. What a stroke would do to the rows is drawn one way, for the pencil, the eraser and a pull alike, as a stroke is in Bemanning: the days that would get FTE as a tint of the row's phase colour with their figures, the days that would lose it hatched in that colour with their figures kept, and what the stroke does on one pill over the days and lines it changes (`strokePill` in `kalender/strokes.ts`, drawn by `kalender/StrokeBox.tsx`, which Bemanning draws its strokes and its selection with too; in the plan the pill is ink, and the blocking alert's chip where the stroke only takes FTE away). A folded level is a row in all of this (`spanClass` in `GridRows.tsx` draws both): its sums are its FTE, drawn as spans in the colour of the phase most of the day's FTE is for, with the window of its rows together (`levelWindows`), and its ends are pulled as a row's are, by its own demand; what a day then gets is shared out over its rows as a number typed there is. It has no cell menu, notes or row details: those belong to a stored row. A press selects nothing by itself: a cell is selected when the mouse is let go on it, a range once the drag reaches another cell, and while a stroke is drawn or an end is pulled no cell is marked as selected; a pull and a stroke leave what was selected as it was. The cell in focus has a thin ring and no tint, and the tint is for the rest of a range. The pencil («Fordel behov») shares what is left of a line's demand over the working days drawn across. Each row shows its window, the build-up or tear-down days of its project in its hall (`domain/windows.ts`), and ✦ («Foreslå plan») fills rows over their windows. A click on a project's name brings its days into view, in narrower columns if they do not fit (`fitSpan` in `kalender/layout.ts`), and lights its halls and bars in the hall calendar for a moment, to say where the view landed (`flash` in `kalender/useProjectHover.ts`); nothing stays lit. The line of a top level stays at the top of the rows while the lines under it scroll past (`levelSections` in `kalender/rows.ts`), and a click on an event's bar in the hall calendar scrolls the rows to its project.
