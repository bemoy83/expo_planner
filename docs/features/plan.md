# Kalender · Plan

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

**Kalender** – the planning workspace. Date header, hall calendar and staffing totals are pinned above the planning rows, and right above the rows sits the planning bar with the tools (`kalender/PlanBar.tsx`): Velg, Fordel behov and Tøm (keys V, B, T, as in Bemanning; Alt-drag gives the eraser for one stroke, and with Velg Shift extends the selection, on a click and along a drag), filter, grouping, the level of detail (1, 2, 3 … by the levels of «Grupper etter», or «Alle»: every branch folded to that level, what was folded by hand let go, `shownLevels` and `foldedTo` in `kalender/rows.ts`; a chevron still folds one branch) and zoom. «Steder» in the page header (`kalender/AreaMenu.tsx`) is a tree of the planner's areas with their halls, to tick the ones to work with, and under it the statuses of Venyou whose bookings count, in both modes: the hall calendar shows the ticked halls and the bookings of the ticked statuses, a project booked in unticked halls or with unticked statuses alone is left out of the rows and of Bemanning's projects, and its FTE out of the need and the demand. The hall calendar has a compact view, as the project lines of Bemanning have (`DensityToggle`): a thin bar per phase without letters or names. What is unticked is kept per browser (`HallFilter` in `domain/areas.ts`). The day under the pointer, over the dates, the top block or the rows, is traced down the date header and the top block, not the rows, and the planning row under it across, both in the one ink `--trace`, a step stronger than a weekend (`dayHover` in `Kalender.tsx`, set by a style variable so no line is drawn again). A click on a date picks the day in focus, the same in every mode (`pickDay` in `Kalender.tsx`): in Plan it lets go of the selected cells, and the day is shared by the modes, as the day of a selected cell is. The date field of the planning bar shows that day, or today where none is picked, and a date put in it is picked the same way and brought into view. In Bemanning and Innleid the day is traced the same way, down the date header, the project lines and the demand, from the pointer over them or over the people and the competences. A right-click on a planning cell opens a menu for the cell and its row, and the panel toggle in the page header slides row details in over the grid (`kalender/RowInspector.tsx`). The row details show the score of the row's project (`kalender/PlanScore.tsx`): what its plan and staffing cost over the whole period, counted in hours, by kind, and the costliest findings (`domain/planner/score.ts`, see below). The Avvik line, named «Mangler / over» in the app (2026-10-23), is a heat map of three answers (`kalender/heat.ts`): red where crew is missing, darker the more; one flat green where it is ok, up to one FTE to spare; ochre where more is over. Its figures are signed, and a zero is not written. It is switched off under Innstillinger. The rows form a hierarchy the planner arranges («Grupper etter»: Prosjekt and Arbeidsfase first, locked (`FIXED_LEVELS`), then Hall/Sted, Kompetanse and Avd. in any order); FTE per day is stored on the rows and every level above sums. A folded level below a project is the deepest line of its branch and takes numbers itself, with no switch to turn on; an open one only sums, and so does a folded project, on its hall-phase strip, since FTE is shared out within a work phase. A number typed on a folded level is shared out by required hours over its rows that can be worked on that day, those whose window holds it or that have no window, and over all of them where none can (`spreadOpen` in `domain/spread.ts`). The days in a row a planning row has FTE are a span, over weekends and holidays without FTE, so that only an empty working day ends it (2026-10-24; the bar is still drawn with its ends at the weekend), and its first and last cell are also its grips, with any tool: a press there dragged sideways pulls that end along the days (`stretchSpan` in `domain/spread.ts`, `pullPreview` in `kalender/strokes.ts`), while a click still selects the cell and a drag up or down selects or draws as from any cell. Outward the new working days get the span's crew, inward the days let go of are cleared and the day at the other end stays; an end stops on the day before the row's next planned day. Shift held while it is pulled, from the press or from later on, stops the end where the row's demand is met; where the row covers its demand already before the pull, Shift keeps the sum and shares it again over the new length instead, and the pill and the status bar say which (2026-10-24). A row whose demand is not known is pulled with Shift as without. Alt is the eraser alone and does nothing to a pull under way. Alt held at the press is the eraser on an end cell as on any other, and a Shift-click on one, or a Shift-drag up or down from it, is what it is on any cell. The cells, the Avvik line and the row's Planlagt and Δ show what the pull would leave while it lasts. What a stroke would do to the rows is drawn one way, for the pencil, the eraser and a pull alike, as a stroke is in Bemanning: the days that would get FTE as a tint of the row's phase colour with their figures, the days that would lose it hatched in that colour with their figures kept, and what the stroke does on one pill over the days and lines it changes (`strokePill` in `kalender/strokes.ts`, drawn by `kalender/StrokeBox.tsx`, which Bemanning draws its strokes and its selection with too; in the plan the pill is ink, and the blocking alert's chip where the stroke only takes FTE away). A folded level is a row in all of this (`spanClass` in `GridRows.tsx` draws both): its sums are its FTE, drawn as spans in the colour of the phase most of the day's FTE is for, with the window of its rows together (`levelWindows`), and its ends are pulled as a row's are, by its own demand, Shift as on a row; what a day then gets is shared out over its rows as a number typed there is. It has no cell menu, notes or row details: those belong to a stored row. A press selects nothing by itself: a cell is selected when the mouse is let go on it, a range once the drag reaches another cell, and while a stroke is drawn or an end is pulled no cell is marked as selected; a pull and a stroke leave what was selected as it was. The cell in focus has a thin ring and no tint, and the tint is for the rest of a range. The pencil («Fordel behov») shares what is left of a line's demand over the working days drawn across. Each row shows its window, the build-up or tear-down days of its project in its hall (`domain/windows.ts`), and ✦ on a level fills its rows over their windows. «✦ Foreslå plan» in the band runs the planning engine (`kalender/EngineDialog.tsx`, `domain/planner/solve.ts`): the days and the permanent staff of every project in the period at once, shown per project with its score now and with the plan, to be taken for the projects the planner picks, as one undo step. He chooses whether the FTE he typed stays or is planned again; the blocks he painted always stay. Where the permanent staff cannot do it all in a window, the window of that project, hall and phase is widened, before anything is left uncovered, onto the working days before the build-up or after the tear-down that the halls stand free in the hall calendar, as many as the hours left over take, and all its work is shared out again over the widened span by the same rules; a widening that saves less than it costs is undone (`freeDaysNear` in `domain/windows.ts`, up to five, every booking counted, hidden or not; the planner can keep the work in the window with «Holdes»): the column «Tidligere» counts them per project, and «Til Venyou» lists them by hall and project for the projects picked, to copy and send so that Venyou blocks the halls. Where his typed FTE stays, it holds the days, so an earlier start is mostly seen with «Kan flyttes». «Til innleie» lists what is left for the projects picked once the engine's permanent staff are on them (`toOrder` in `domain/planner/toOrder.ts`): per competence the hours, the days, the most people a day (a normal day each, a call-out of three hours at least) and the suppliers who offer it, or a mark where none does. The engine orders nothing; the same hours are what remains in Innleid, where the planner orders with the brush. The days and blocks the engine wrote are marked (`AllocationRow.engine`, `Assignment.source: 'suggested'`), so the next run plans them again; a day or a block he changes is his own from then on (`ownChanged`). A click on a project's name brings its days into view, in narrower columns if they do not fit (`fitSpan` in `kalender/layout.ts`), and lights its halls and bars in the hall calendar for a moment, to say where the view landed (`flash` in `kalender/useProjectHover.ts`); nothing stays lit. The line of a top level stays at the top of the rows while the lines under it scroll past (`levelSections` in `kalender/rows.ts`), and a click on an event's bar in the hall calendar scrolls the rows to its project.

**The score of a plan** (`domain/planner/score.ts`, step 2 of the planning engine) says what a plan with its staffing costs, in hours and never in kroner: a permanent hour costs nothing, since the permanent staff are there anyway. The costs, with their weights in `WEIGHTS`: an hour nobody covers (10), an hired hour (1 for the competence easiest to replace, the last on Kompetanser, to 1,5 for the hardest, the first; a hired person is paid for three hours at least), overtime (0,5 for a person who works any that day, 0,8 an hour for the first 1,5 hours and 2 an hour past them), a person's switch of competence (1, not along the team's chain on Kompetanser), hall (0,5) or project (0,1) between blocks no more than three days apart, a project's day of work in a hall outside its window (3), a day a stage starts before the stage before it or ends after the one after it, the other way in a tear-down (1), and a person short of the fewest per hall (2). Orders that wait for an answer count as cover. The weights agree with the planner's choices between pairs of plans (2026-10-25), which are tests in `score.test.ts`.
