# Resultat

**Resultat** (`ui/resultat/`) – the season as a dashboard, under a band of its own (indigo), built from the handoff `design_docs/design_handoff_resultat/` (not in git) on 2026-10-23. A row per project number that has a booking shown in the Kalender, planned FTE or a budget, ordered by build-up (`seasonRows` in `domain/result.ts`). A switch in the band shows Alle, Kommende, Pågår or Avsluttet, by the project's first and last booked day against today (`seasonStatus`; without bookings, its planned days), kept per browser.

- **Six figures** over the projects shown (`ResultFigures.tsx`): the budget, the plan with the overhead of Innstillinger (`Settings.overhead`, 10 % by default), the plan against the budget, the planned hours per day of build-up and of tear-down with what the permanent staff can work per day (`dayAverages`, `staffPerDay`), what is staffed, and the actual hours (empty until a source of them is agreed).
- **The matrix** (`ResultMatrix.tsx`): per project its hall days in all five phases of the venue, counted once over its halls; a tile per hall it has booked, for the halls ticked under «Steder» in the Kalender (the same choice, `hallFilter`, changed on either page), with the rest counted under «Andre»; its plan with overhead as a bar against its budget, each project scaled to its own budget so the budget's tick stands at the same place in every row; its planned hours per day of build-up and tear-down against what the permanent staff can work in their normal time on the project's own days (`staffHours` in `domain/staffing/time.ts`), a bar to the right where the plan needs more; and the plan against the budget. A line «I dag» runs between the projects started and those to come. The heading, the sums and the legend stay in view while the rows scroll.
- **Read against the budget:** + is over the budget and − under it, on the page alone; the domain keeps the deviation as budget less the plan (`deviation`). Over is in the colour `--over`; nothing is green.
- **The panel of a project** (`ProjectResult.tsx`), opened by a click on its row: its budget, typed here (stored per project number in `Workspace.budgets`, and read under «Fil» with `import/budsjettFile.ts`, which also reads the budget workbook's «Prosjektnr.» and «Budsjett arbeidstimer totalt»); the plan, the deviation and the actual hours; its halls and phase days with its hours per day against the staff; and per competence its demand under «Planlagt» beside its plan and what is staffed (`projectHours`, `projectStaffing`). «Bemannet» is what Bemanning and the confirmed orders of Innleid staff it with, tagged and shared out (`seasonStaffing`), with what is tagged and what is shared in the tooltip.
- **Følg opp** (`FollowUp.tsx`): the projects shown that need the planner (`followUps`): a plan over budget, actual hours over budget, a start within two weeks with less than nine tenths of the plan staffed, a budget without a plan, and no budget. Its foot counts the events without a project number, with a link to Prosjekter, and the hours staffed beyond what the projects lack or on demand without a number.
- **Actual hours** are prepared for and not read in (the planner's decision, 2026-10-23: the data is not ready): a row has `actual` and `actualDeviation`, always null for now, and the bar, the columns «Faktisk mot budsjett» and «Faktisk mot plan» and the figure show only when a project has them.
