# Resultat

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

**Resultat** (`ui/resultat/`) – the season against the budget, under a band of its own («Kobber», copper): a `DataTable` with a row per project number that has a booking shown in the Kalender, planned FTE or a budget, ordered by build-up (`seasonRows` in `domain/result.ts`), with the sums of the rows shown in a line under them (`foot` of a `Column`). Per project its hall days per phase, counted once over its halls, its halls, the budget, the planned hours (FTE × normaltid) and those with the overhead of Innstillinger added (`Settings.overhead`, 10 % by default), the deviation, budget less the plan with overhead, red where the plan is over, and the planned hours of montering and demontering. The budget is one total of work hours per project, overhead included, typed in the row or read under «Fil» (`import/budsjettFile.ts`, which also reads the budget workbook's «Prosjektnr.» and «Budsjett arbeidstimer totalt»); it is stored per project number (`Workspace.budgets`) and follows a project given another number. A click on a project slides in its hours per competence and phase beside its demand under «Planlagt» (`ProjectResult.tsx`, `projectHours`). «Bemannet» is what Bemanning and the confirmed orders of Innleid staff the project with, tagged and shared out (`seasonStaffing`), and «Gjenstår» the plan less that; the hours on demand without a number and those beyond what all projects lack are written under the table, and the panel has them per competence and phase (`projectStaffing`). Events without a project number have no row; the page says how many.
