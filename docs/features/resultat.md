# Resultat

**Resultat** (`ui/resultat/`) – the season as a ledger on the canvas, under a band of its own (indigo), built from the handoff `design_docs/design_handoff_resultat_v2/` (not in git) on 2026-10-24, which put the page of 2026-10-23 back in the look of the Kalender. A row per project number that has a booking shown in the Kalender, planned FTE or a budget, ordered by build-up (`seasonRows` in `domain/result.ts`). A switch in the band shows Alle, Kommende, Pågår or Avsluttet, by the project's first and last booked day against today (`seasonStatus`; without bookings, its planned days), kept per browser.

- **Six figures** over the projects shown, as the lower half of the band, a step toward the band's line (`ResultFigures.tsx`): the budget, the plan with the overhead of Innstillinger (`Settings.overhead`, 10 % by default), the plan against the budget, what is staffed, the planned hours per day of build-up and of tear-down with what the permanent staff can work per day (`dayAverages`, `staffPerDay`), and the actual hours (empty until a source of them is agreed).
- **The ledger** (`ResultLedger.tsx`): the rows in sections, Avsluttet, Pågår and Kommende, each with its count and its sums; a sum row and the heading stay in view. The project's column (its name, number and halls) is held to the left and five columns of figures to the right; only the season's timeline between them moves sideways, a day 12 px wide, every other month a step darker as the weekends of the Kalender. The timeline follows the rows (`useFollowScroll`): the project a quarter down the rows in view sets it, its first day 12 % in, panning toward the next project's as it passes; it opens with today 30 % in. On it a project has its booked phases as one bar in the phase colours of the hall calendar, over all its halls, with its days beside it; today is one line down the rows, under the sections. The sum row draws the planned hours per day of the projects shown, summed where they overlap, from the plan's FTE (`plannedByDay`, `seasonDays`), grey up to what the permanent staff can work that day (dashed) and in the colour of what is over above it.
- **The figures of a row**: its plan with overhead as a bar against its budget, each project scaled to its own budget so the budget's tick stands at the same place in every row; its planned hours per day of build-up and tear-down against what the permanent staff can work in their normal time on the project's own days (`staffHours` in `domain/staffing/time.ts`); and the plan against the budget, with the actual hours against the budget and against the plan where there are any.
- **Read against the budget:** + is over the budget and − under it, on the page alone; the domain keeps the deviation as budget less the plan (`deviation`). A deviation is read after the fact and nothing warns: it is on a tile of the Kalender's heat map graded by its share, the same over and under (`reviewGrade`: within 3 % the green of ok, within 10 % and 25 % ochre, beyond that red; `GRADE_TILES` in `ui/kalender/heat.ts`). The hours over the budget, and over the staff in the sum row, are in `--res-over`; the plan is a neutral grey and the actual hours graphite.
- **The panel of a project** (`ProjectResult.tsx`), opened by a click on its row: its budget, typed here (stored per project number in `Workspace.budgets`, and read under «Fil» with `import/budsjettFile.ts`, which also reads the budget workbook's «Prosjektnr.» and «Budsjett arbeidstimer totalt»); the plan, the deviation and the actual hours; its halls and phase days with its hours per day against the staff; and per competence its demand under «Planlagt» beside its plan and what is staffed (`projectHours`, `projectStaffing`). «Bemannet» is what Bemanning and the confirmed orders of Innleid staff it with, tagged and shared out (`seasonStaffing`), with what is tagged and what is shared in the tooltip.
- **Følg opp** (`FollowUp.tsx`): the projects shown that need the planner (`followUps`): a plan over budget, actual hours over budget, a start within two weeks with less than nine tenths of the plan staffed, a budget without a plan, and no budget. Its foot counts the events without a project number, with a link to Prosjekter, and the hours staffed beyond what the projects lack or on demand without a number.
- **Actual hours** are prepared for and not read in (the planner's decision, 2026-10-23: the data is not ready): a row has `actual` and `actualDeviation`, always null for now, and the bar, the columns «Faktisk mot budsjett» and «Faktisk mot plan» and the figure show only when a project has them.
