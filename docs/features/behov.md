# Behov

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

**Behov** – the demand, in two views behind a switch in the page header, which has a band of its own («Lyng», heather), and with the projects down the left (`ui/behov/ProjectRail.tsx`). **Visma · gjennomgang** (`VismaView.tsx`): a Visma line counts in the demand that is planned with («Planlagt») by itself once it gives hours and has a hall; the planner resolves what a line lacks, or leaves it out (`excluded` on its override). Where each line stands is worked out in `domain/vismaReview.ts`: «Trenger avklaring», «Med» or «Utelatt». A work type and a hall are picked in the row (`SplitPick.tsx`: what needs the planner is one button, `NeedButton`, raised with a dot of attention, showing the offer with a «?» or what is missing; a click opens the list on `PickField` with the offer first and marked, so Enter takes it). The lists of Behov stand in groups under headings (`PickField`'s `groups`, values in `behov/picks.ts`): for a hall the offer, the halls the project has booked with their days, places of several halls, the other halls, and the booked halls for this project alone, or by cause in «Avklar» (`Avklar.tsx`), a panel that resolves a Hall/Sted text, an article text or a missing rate for all its lines at once. The hours per competence and basis are in a panel of their own (`HoursSummary.tsx`). **Egen registrering** (`EgenView.tsx`): the planner's own lines, for work not sold through Visma. A form on top (`OwnLineForm.tsx`) where project › hall › avd. stays set between lines, hours typed in short («3p 2d», `parseHours` in `domain/ownDemand.ts`), «Planlagt» or «Utkast» (saved, not counted), and a preview of what the line does to its Kalender row; the lines under it by project and hall, edited in place (`OwnLines.tsx`). «Erstatt» on a Visma line leaves it out and fills the form from it; the own line keeps the key in `replaces`. The rail lists the projects by their build-up (`projectTiming`). «Fra i fjor» offers the own lines of the project the year before, found by the series of its number, that are not registered this year (`fromLastYear`); «Ikke i år» is stored on last year's line (`notIn`). A work type picked in «Avklar» can be remembered for a Visma article text (`KpiConfig.byText`), listed on KPI. The redesign was built in five slices (the Claude Doc «Behov: build plan», 2026-10-14).
