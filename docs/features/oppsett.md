# Oppsett: Produkttyper, Tekstregler og Utelatte artikler

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

**Oppsett** (`ui/kpi/Oppsett.tsx`) – how the orders are read, in three pages under a band of its own («Kobber», copper, Oppsett's alone), with undo and «Fil» in its header since the KPI file holds all of it, and the page's key figures as the band's lower half (none where its list is empty). The pages lie on the canvas as Behov's do: what is worked in on a neutral strip, the table edge to edge under it, and no sum line, and a toast with «Angre» for each change. **Produkttyper** (`ProductTypes.tsx`; on the band the number of types and how many lack setup, and the hours of Montering and Demontering of those that lack nothing, `setupHours` in `domain/visma.ts`): a line per product type (`kpiTypes` in `domain/kpi.ts`), those that still lack setup first under «Mangler oppsett», then a group per competence with the hours it gives. What a type lacks is written beside its name and asked for by a raised chip in the cell it is filled in. The unit in use is a switch where there are several, and the units fold out under the type with their rates, what they count and the hours they would give; the competence is picked from a list (`SplitPick`) or typed as a new one; «Gir timer» is quantity ÷ rate over the exports read in, before Effekt (`yieldByType` in `domain/visma.ts`), or «ukjent». A strip over the table says how a line becomes hours. **Tekstregler** (`TextRules.tsx`; on the band the rules and the order lines they give a type): the product type an article text of lines without one is read as (`KpiConfig.byText`), made in «Avklar» on Behov and changed or forgotten here, with the lines each gives (`linesByText`). **Utelatte artikler** (`ExcludedArticles.tsx`; on the band the articles and the order lines they keep out): article numbers whose booking lines are not read at all, in any project (`KpiConfig.excludedArticles`, `isCounted` in `domain/kpi.ts`), for what is ordered but is no work for the crew, added from the numbers seen in the exports; they go with the KPI file on a sheet of their own, and Behov says how many lines they hold out. A card in «Avklar» shows a booking line per art.nr behind it, as it reads in the orders, with «Utelat» to add the number to the list (`articleSamples` in `domain/vismaReview.ts`).

A product type can have a **Trinn** of its own on Produkttyper, where its work comes in a build-up otherwise than its competence's: built-up floor and laminate are carpentry on the floor. Without one it follows its competence's stage from Mannskap › Kompetanser (`stageOfType` in `domain/kpi.ts`). The KPI file has it as «Trinn»; a file without the column keeps the stages set.

**Planleggingsmotor** (`ui/kpi/EngineRules.tsx`) holds the rules of «✦ Foreslå plan», open to the planner (2026-10-25): the order of the stages in a build-up, moved with a grip or arrows (a tear-down takes them the other way round), with what each holds, which competences are on it and which product types have it as their own; the two stages outside the order, running (banner) and extra (meeting rooms, seating); and the weights of the score, each in hours against a permanent hour, with what it counts and its default. They are stored in the settings (`Settings.engine`, only what differs from the defaults), and the engine and the score read them from there (`stageOrderOf`, `weightsOf`). «Tilbake til standard» clears them.
