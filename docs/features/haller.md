# Haller: VenYou, Prosjekter og Hallregler

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

**VenYou** (the hall ledger, `ui/haller/`) – every hall booking from Venyou, with a tick for whether it shows in the Kalender. The project number of an event is shown, not typed: it is read from Prosjekter.

**Prosjekter** – the projects: a number each, and every name a project goes by in the sources, a row per project. The number is the id that demand, orders and planning rows point at. An event in Venyou gets the number of the project that carries its name in its year. Events without a project are listed first, each with what it is offered: the project of its year whose name is like its own and whose days do not speak against it (`nameLikeness`, `nameWithin`, `ProjectRow.match`), else the number of its year after the project it was another year («27231» after «26231», `ProjectRow.series`, only where that number is free), else a number of its own (the year and three letters, «26VAM»). One click takes the offer, or the number of the project it belongs to is typed. A number that comes with orders and is like a project under another number can take over from it (`withNumberTakingOver`). A project given another number takes its planning rows and the planner's own demand lines along.

**Hallregler** (`ui/behov/HallRules.tsx`) – the rules that place a Hall/Sted text of the demand in a hall, all of them the planner's own: places that stand for several halls (named, renamed, and their halls ticked in a list), words that mean a place, in the order they are tried, and the choices made for single texts. The page also holds the areas («Områder»: a name and its halls, as «NV HALLS»), which place nothing: they are what «Steder» in the Kalender lists the halls by. One part is shown at a time, picked in the bar of the page (`Part` in `HallRules.tsx`), so no table is scrolled past to reach another: the texts that lack a hall, the places, the rules for words and the choices for single texts, which are last since their list grows long, and apart from them the areas. The first part is the texts of the demand that no rule places, a row per text with its lines and hours, to place there with one choice or by the offer it has. The panel that tries a text and shows the order the rules are tried in, with the lines each step places (`PlacingPanel.tsx`), stands at the right of the rules; a click on a text tries it there. Reached from Behov, where «Regel» says which of them placed a line.
