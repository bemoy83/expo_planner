# Arbeidsplan: dagsplan og ukeplan

What the page does, as built. Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-23 so that it is read when work touches this part, not in every session; the rules of the code stay in CLAUDE.md.

**Arbeidsplan** (`ui/dagsplan/`, `domain/dayPlan.ts`, `domain/weekPlan.ts`, `design_docs/ukeplan/`): the two printouts of Bemanning, opened from «Arbeidsplan» in the page header of Bemanning and Innleid, and by a right-click on a date for the day plan; one dialog with «Dag» and «Uke», arrows that step a day or a week, and «Skriv ut», which prints the open sheet alone, always in the light palette (`.palette-light` in `tokens.css`, the frame in `print.css`, the sheets in `dagsplan.css`). The **day plan** has a card per project that works the day (its places and halls, the phase with day n of N, coverage per competence worked out for the project, the notes of its planning rows), then the people as the day is read out at the start-up meeting: by place and then competence (`groups`), each competence with its people as whole days (paid hours over the normal day, so no one is counted twice), what waits, the project's coverage, and what it is to do there (`dayWork`: the two product types of the demand that give the most hours in the phase, with their quantities over the whole phase, «+N til» for the rest, and «ca. N % i dag», the day's planned FTE over the phase's); a fitter with work in two groups stands in both, the whole day on the bar with the group's own blocks full and the rest in outline, and a later part of the day marked by a line before the name; the suppliers sit in their project's place, and those with blocks for no project and who is away or without a block close it. The **week plan** is by project and place, no hours and no names: the phase over the days and one line per competence of the project, with a bar over each stretch of days it is planned (a gap is a pause), however many places and departments its rows have (named on the bar, the places only where the project has two or more halls that week), and flags for days that lack crew or wait for an answer. Where a block's work is stands on the sheets whole: `dealPlaces` in `domain/workPlace.ts` gives each block one place (set, or worked out and then ≈ where it could have been another), and «Sett stedene» stores it on the blocks in one undo step (`withPlacesSet`). Nothing of the sheets is stored.
