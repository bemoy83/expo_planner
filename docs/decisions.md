# Decisions and what was built

The parts of the app as they were built, slice by slice: what the designs asked for, where they disagreed with the app or with themselves, and what was chosen. Moved from docs/TODO.md on 2026-10-23; what is still open is listed there. What each page does now is in [features/](features/README.md).

## Bemanning: a mode of the Kalender, to be tested by the user

Bemanning was built as a tab of its own from `design_docs/bemanning/`, one week at a time. It is now a mode of the Kalender on the same timeline, over the whole period, built from `design_docs/bemanning-v2/` (all seven phases, 2026-10-09). The tab, its week picker, the week editor, the «Rad» timelines and the absence dialog are gone; the rules, the storage and the undo steps are the same.

What v2 added: the switch between Plan and Bemanning with the zoom between them, the days Bemanning opens on, the projects in view, the demand per competence in a detailed and a compact form, the people as lines of the grid with painting and erasing, one person's open hours with blocks that are dragged to another day, a day's blocks dragged between days and people in the folded rows, copy and paste of days, the Person panel with overtime per week and absence as periods, the overtime limit, and the settings «Navn på blokker» and the overtime limit under Innstillinger. Of the first handoff's «Later» list, overtime is fed into the Kalender's staffing lines. Deferred in `design_docs/bemanning-v2/DEFERRED.md` and since built: hired staff (Innleid, 2026-10-13), and demand per project with tagging (2026-10-21). Still untouched: trade crews, the other views of the side panel, the row details' Bemanning section, automatic suggestions, and showing moved hours in the Kalender.

Where v2's package disagreed with the app or with itself, and what was done

- The label column is the Kalender's 460 px in both modes; the package says 400.
- A click on a person's name opens the Person panel. The arrow before the name, a double-click on a day and E open the hours; the package says both of the name.
- There is no «Ledig kapasitet» line (removed 2026-10-14, the user's choice): what can't be covered shows on the competence's day, and a day's free hours with its competence in the day's popover. The package's header does not have it either.
- The right-click menu keeps «Mal hel dag med …», which the package's list leaves out.
- «N uløste · Fjern» is in the planning bar only, not in the page header as well.
- A person's hours in the label are those of the week of the day in focus while it is in view, else of the week of the first workday in view.
- The two modes share the timeline (the user, 2026-10-09): on a switch, the day at the left edge of the one is the day at the left edge of the other, and only the width of a day changes. Bemanning keeps the width it was last seen in. The package's order (the chosen project, the days last seen, ten days from the plan's edge, R18 and R29) is not followed; «Tilpass prosjekt» and a choice of project in Bemanning still fit the days to the project. A page that opens in Bemanning opens on the days last seen there; one that opens in the plan opens on the day in focus.
- The days that are selected show as a tint with no outline (the package's decision 4); the outline shows only while a selection is being dragged. The package's §8b asks for an outline on both.
- The projects in view are those with a booking in the halls ticked under «Steder» and with FTE planned somewhere in the period: a project without has nothing to staff (the user, 2026-10-09). The list is at most six lines tall in both forms; projects that do not fit are counted as «+n» beside the heading, with their names in its tooltip, so no work is hidden unseen. A project with FTE but no booking in a shown hall has no line; its hours are in the demand per competence.
- The hours that remain, per competence («t igjen») and in the page header, are those of the days in view (the user, 2026-10-09). The package sums the week; summed over the whole period they counted every project read in and said nothing of what is being staffed. What each project lacks is on its line under a competence in focus (`buildProjectBalance`, 2026-10-21).
- Each mode keeps how far down its rows were scrolled, until the page is reloaded: the plan is found again where it was left, and the people open at the top the first time. While the switch zooms, the projects listed are those of the days the plan shows at its own width, so the rows do not move as the zoom passes through fewer days.
- The date header is pinned in both modes. The block under it is always pinned too, however much of the grid it covers: its sections are how the planner finds the way and stay within reach, and a layout where all of them are open at once is not a known way of working (the user, 2026-10-09). It gives way only where it would leave a person's open hours no room, or the rows less than three lines: it then moves up by what does not fit as the rows scroll (`tucked` in `kalender/useGridViewport.ts`). In the plan the hall calendar stays where it is and the staffing lines slide in under it; in Bemanning the upper lines of the projects go first.
- The compact demand line starts as a tint and fills solid from the left as the day is covered (the user, 2026-10-09). The package has it the other way: solid for what remains, from the right.

Where the code is: the types in `domain/types.ts`; the rules in `domain/staffing/` (once `domain/staffing.ts`) (R1–R15 of RULES.md, with the checks D1–D23 of ACCEPTANCE.md in `staffing/staffing.test.ts`) and `domain/competences.ts`; people, absence, assignments and moved hours as records of the workspace (`store/records.ts`), and competence styles with them; undo through `updateStaffing` in the store; the mode in `ui/bemanning/`, held together by `BemanningScope.tsx`, and the tabs Personell and Kompetanser in `ui/personell/`; styles in `styles/bemanning.css`.

The mockup's invented people and week 42 2026 are read by the tests only (`test/staffingFixture.ts`, from `design_docs/bemanning/fixtures/`). The app has no way to load them; the menu item that did is removed.

Choices made where the handoff was open or disagreed with itself

- The lunch break is unpaid on weekends and holidays too, as in the mockup: Saturday 08:00–12:00 counts 3,5 hours. RULES.md's example for R2 says 4.
- A full-day paint leaves a gap that is all lunch alone, so a day painted as two half days stays two blocks.
- Hours show quarter hours in full (2,75 t); the mockup rounds to one decimal.
- The list of competences is every competence text on the product types, the demand, the planning rows and the people. The demand of Bemanning lists only the competences an active person has, plus any other with demand or assigned hours in the period. Those with neither demand nor assigned hours in the period are folded under one line, «Uten behov», which opens on a click; the competence in focus stays in the list. The keys 1–9 follow the competences people have, in the order set on Personell.
- A competence's name on Personell is how it is shown; it does not rename the competence on the product types. A competence gets its colour and place when it first appears, and these are stored at the first edit of any competence.
- The day in focus is shared with the Kalender through the `planningFocus` preference: the Kalender opens on it and marks it in the date header, and the day of the cell the planner stands on becomes it.
- A holiday on a weekday is shown as a day off, like the weekend. Every day has the same width; the first build had narrow weekends.
- The right-click menu of a day also paints a full day with one of the person's competences. «Fravær …» opens the form in the Person panel, for a stretch of days and a part of the day; «Tilbake fra» ends an absence on the day before. No reason for absence is stored (2026-10-19): the plan needs only there or away, and the reason belongs in HR.
- A block whose person is away or lacks the competence never counts. It is «uløst» only while hours of its competence remain that day (`openUnresolved`). Once others cover the day, it is shown as a faint trace and is no longer counted or flagged; the user asked for this, where RULES.md R7 keeps it unresolved. If the person comes back, the block counts again and the day shows a surplus.
- «N uløste · Fjern» counts and removes the open unresolved blocks of the whole period. The faint traces are removed by hand.
- Moved hours can be taken back from the popover of the day they were moved to.
- Hours moved from a Friday land on the Saturday; the user has confirmed this.
- The Kalender's «Faste» is the number of people on Mannskap › Faste (`planningSettings` in `domain/calc.ts`). The number under Innstillinger stands in only until the first person is entered. A person's absence does not lower «Faste»; it shows on its own line, «Fravær faste».
- The competence colours include an orange and an amber, and the primary buttons are orange; the user keeps them for now.

Gaps

- People are entered by hand or read from the app's own Personell file; there is no reader for another staff register. People cannot be reordered. Absence is entered in Bemanning's Person panel only, not from Personell.
- Competences that are in use cannot be hidden or removed; one that nothing names can be deleted on Kompetanser.
- The preview «−N» shows for folded days only, not while drawing in a person's open hours.
- A painted day never replaces what is there (v2); the question «Fyll resten / Erstatt» is gone, and so is the replace mode of `paintDays`.
- With many competences the detailed demand leaves the people little room on a laptop screen, since it stays pinned; the compact form fits. Which of them should be the default is open (v2's open question 1).
- The zoom between the modes draws no line again on the way: the width of a day is the style variable `--col-w`, which every cell and bar is laid out by, and a frame of the zoom writes it and the place of the left edge to the page (`zoomStep` in `kalender/useGridViewport.ts`). The days the zoom passes are drawn once, before it starts (`heldColumns` in `kalender/zoom.ts`). Timed in the development build, 1 260 cells: a frame took 15–25 ms of drawing before, with some of 50–75 ms, and takes about 7 ms of layout now. What a line writes in a day by the room it has (the weekday's letters, the week numbers, the figures of the heat map, the phase letters of the hall bars) is that of the plan's own width for the whole zoom. The frame that swaps the rows of the plan for the people, at the end of the way in, took 225–250 ms and takes 105–130 ms: the balance of the period goes through each row's own days and not the period's (`buildBalance`, 80 ms of it in the development build), the demand lines are handed the same handlers every time and are not drawn twice (30 ms), and fewer of Bemanning's wide days are drawn out of sight (`overscanCols` in `kalender/layout.ts`). What is left of it is the first drawing of the people, the demand and the bar. The frame that puts the plan back, at the start of the way out, is as it was, 85–105 ms. «Tilpass prosjekt» in Bemanning goes to a project in one step, without the zoom, when its days are more than three screens away.
- The days Bemanning fits to do not make room for an open Person panel; the panel lies over the last days.
- A new block does not animate in, as the mockup's does: the lines are drawn again while scrolling, and every block would animate then.
- A day's blocks are dragged as one in the folded rows; one block of several is moved in the open hours.
- Painting, dragging, the zoom and the panel are checked by hand in the browser; the rules behind them have tests (`staffing.test.ts`, `zoom.test.ts`, `projectsInView.test.ts`, `dayCell.test.ts`).
- With a competence in focus the people who lack it are dimmed under a divider, not hidden.
- While a person's hours are open and would not fit under the top block, the block gives way by as much as they need: the upper lines of the projects, then of the demand, are out of sight until the people are scrolled back to the top.
- Reading back from IndexedDB after a reload is checked by hand in the browser. The main gestures of Bemanning (the brush by click, stroke and Shift, the eraser and Alt, a move, copy and paste, a run pulled out and back, the keys 1–9, Escape, the Person panel, the switch to Innleid) have component tests on a fake IndexedDB (`bemanning/Bemanning.test.tsx`, 2026-10-23); the open hours, absence and the demand lines are still checked by hand.

## Innleid: built, to be tested by the user

Built from `design_docs/innleid/` in the six slices of the Claude Doc «Innleid: build plan» (2026-10-13). The user's answers: only confirmed orders count in «Tilgjengelig»; the Kalender's Avvik changes to the three states (under, ok, over) with slice 2; the suppliers are a tab made from Personell.

A competence a supplier has counts as in use, and is renamed with the rest.
- **Slice 2 is in: Innleid read-only, and the Kalender's Avvik in three states.** Bemanning has the parts «Faste · Innleid»; Innleid shows the Avvik per day in hours and a line per competence with what is missing or over, on a fjord blue band. Checked in the browser with invented data read in as a backup, on a dev server of its own (another port has its own database): bars for confirmed and waiting orders, the clock, the chips of the bar, and the switch back to Faste on the same days.
  - The competences listed are those with hours that remain or with orders somewhere in the period; their sums in the label count the days in view, and «N dager udekket» the whole period.
  - The keys of Faste (V, B, T, 1 to 9) are at rest in Innleid.
  - The days Innleid opens on are those of Faste.
- **Slice 3 is in: ordering and answers.** Days are picked on a competence by a click, a drag or Shift+click, and its label becomes the editor: «+ Leverandør» with the count filled in, supplier, shift or own times, people, answer, remove and «Fyll». ## Resultatside: agreed design

Agreed with the user 2026-10-18, and built the same day as the tab Resultat, to be tested by the user. The workbook's version and the reasoning are in the project's notes (`plans/resultatside-concept.md`).

- In the workbook, Resultatside is one row per event for the whole season. It compares a budget of work hours with the planned FTE × normaltid × 1.10. The budget is one total per project, typed by hand in a separate workbook («Budsjett arbeidstimer Production.xlsx») and split there by fixed ratios: 90/10 for overhead and 56/10/24 for montering, service and demontering.
- **Budget.** It is a static number per project number: a total of hours, typed by the planner, that the total planned hours are compared against. It has no split by phase or competence. It is the planner's own table, so it has «Fil» with export and import, which can also read the budget workbook's «Prosjektnr.» and «Budsjett arbeidstimer totalt». It follows a project that is given another number.
- **Overhead.** The budget holds overhead, while the plan counts direct work only. So the planned hours are compared with overhead added: planned × (1 + overhead). Overhead is one percentage under Innstillinger, 10 % by default, and it is named in the column heading.
- **The page.** A tab «Resultat» after Behov, with one season table (a `DataTable`) and a row per project, ordered by build-up. The columns are:
  - Project.
  - Halldager per phase, from the hall ledger.
  - The halls, as a short list.
  - Budsjett, Planlagt and Planlagt inkl. overhead.
  - Avvik and Avvik %.
  - Planlagt montering and demontering.

  The totals of the season go at the bottom. A click on a project slides in its details (`SidePanel`): planned hours per competence × phase, beside the project's demand.
- **Planned hours** are the Plan's FTE × hoursPerDay. Since project tagging (2026-10-21), «Bemannet» and «Gjenstår» set Bemanning's hours and the confirmed orders beside them (`seasonStaffing`).
- **Left out, to start.** These are in the workbook but not on the page:
  - Historikk (see «History for earlier years»).
  - The Service split: the app has no service phase.
  - Planned hours per work type: the app's rows are per competence.
  - An X column per hall, and the week and month columns: the table sorts and filters by date.

Choices made in the build, for the user to confirm

- Avvik is the budget less the plan with overhead: positive is what is left of the budget, negative is over it and red. Nothing is green.
- A row per project number that has a booking shown in the Kalender, FTE planned or a budget; every year read in, with a column «År» to filter by. Events without a number have no row, and the page counts them with a link to Prosjekter. Bookings hidden in the VenYou tab are left out; «Steder» in the Kalender is not followed.
- Halldager are calendar days of the venue's phases A, E and D, each day counted once however many halls have it. Moving in and out are not shown.
- A budget of nought, or an empty field, is no budget, as in the budget workbook. A file with a project on several rows gives it their sum.
- The sums at the bottom are of the rows the filters let through. Avvik and Avvik % there are over the projects that have a budget alone.
- Planning rows without a work phase count in Planlagt and not in montering or demontering; rows without a project number are not counted.
