Kalender: design rules

Lines are information, not decoration. A line only exists where the eye moves along it to read numbers. Everything else is separated by tint, spacing or type.

1 · Mode band

The header and toolbar form one band in the mode color. Two lines frame the band, with no line between header and toolbar. Mode color appears nowhere else.

1Date header: vertical day lines (they define the columns). One soft line under the whole header.
2Section row: neutral tint, no lines. Weekend a step darker than the tint.
3Readout row: no lines, label right-aligned against the numbers.
4Top level: the only row with the strong line. Its own fill; weekend a step darker.
5Phase subtotal: faint line above, no fill, muted. Doesn't look editable.
6Editable rows: day lines plus a row line. The first row closes the block with a line on top.
7Last row drops its bottom line; the next group's line takes over. Never two lines on top of each other.

4 · Never do this

Every one of these has happened before.

Invisible row linesA transparent border with width on .krow/.ksec opens a 1px gap, and weekends and the today line bleed through. Use width 0.
See-through labelsSticky .klab must always have a solid background, or content shows through when you scroll sideways.
Translucent weekendscolor-mix percentages must add up to 100. Weekends are opaque and fill the full row height.
Mode color in the gridMode hue belongs only in the band. Section rows and the grid stay neutral.
Lines on non-editable rowsIf you can't type into it, it has no day lines.
Strong line as a closing line--g-top only marks the start of a new top level.

5 · Today line

One continuous 2px line in the accent color. It sits above grid lines, weekends, bars and selection. It sits below sticky labels, the mode band and the planning header.
